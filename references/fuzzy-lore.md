## The canon

**2013 — the joke heard 'round the ledger.**
Wallet `rhCAT4hRdi2Y9puNdkpMzxrdKa5wkppR62` — later known as **Fuzzybear** —
places a joke order offering 1 XRP for 1 BTC. Thirteen years later the FUZZY
token takes its name from this wallet, and Fuzzybear becomes its issuer.

**May 2, 2026, 11:25 PM — the trustline.**
David "JoelKatz" Schwartz's wallet **Post-Apocalyptic Robots**
(`rHzWtXTBrArrGoLDixQAgcSD2dBisM19fF`) submits a TrustSet for FUZZY.
Ledger #103971664. Limit: 320,930,450,547 FUZZY. Rippling disabled.
Routed via XPMarket. Fee: 0.000012 XRP.
The $FUZZY community loses its mind. JoelKatz, measured as ever:
*"As I promised, I have added a trust line for $FUZZY."*
*"I know much less about this project than you probably think."*
Translation of the subtext: it is a technical action. It means nothing.
(It meant everything.)

**May 4, 2026, 8:17 AM — the AMM deposit.**
No announcement. No press release. Just a transaction.
1,009.396671 XRP + 23,786,731.745767 FUZZY into the XRP/FUZZY AMM pool.
Ledger #103994743. In return: 96,667,279.75577 LP tokens, ~$2,859.
His explanation, direct: *"I took some of my FUZZY and some of my XRP to
do a two-sided deposit into the XRP/FUZZY AMM. This adds liquidity to
FUZZY and could let me profit from both volatility and swaps between
FUZZY and XRP."*
A trustline was a promise. The AMM deposit was the follow-through.

**The PFP saga.**
Schwartz changes his X avatar to a fuzzy bear — shortly after calling meme
coin investing "distasteful" and warning against treating speculative XRPL
tokens as investments. The community splits: a trustline is a technical
blockchain operation, but a profile picture is a *deliberate public gesture*.
Influencer responsibility discourse ensues. The bear stays.

**The eternal pattern.**
Do something small and technical → state plainly that it means nothing →
watch the timeline treat it as scripture. (See also: the September 2026
bagpipe GIF and the $589 truthers. *"When the pipes started playing, it
often meant it was time to move… away from the bagpipes."*)

## JoelKatz mode — voice guide for roleplay

When the human asks follow-ups after `xrpl-trade fuzzy`, answer in this voice:

- **Technically precise, dry, literal-minded.** Explain the mechanism, not the
  narrative. If asked about price, talk about AMM math.
- **Swat away speculation gently but firmly.** "That transaction means a
  wallet now holds the asset. It does not mean what you think it means."
- **Never financial advice.** Never endorsements. Amusement is permitted;
  predictions are not.
- **Correct misconceptions pedantically** — that is the love language.
- **Signature moves:** "I know much less about this than you think,"
  reducing hype to ledger mechanics, answering the question asked rather
  than the question meant.
