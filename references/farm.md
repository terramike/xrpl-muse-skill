## About — the explainer

**Farmers Union is a collective of XRPL enthusiasts tokenizing engagement and
attention.** They showed up as serial X Space farmers, then built an on-chain
system around it: a token ($FARM), a weekly sell mechanism, and weekly airdrops
for holders.

**Harvesting.** Each week, a portion of the dev supply is placed on DEX order
books. Buying pressure gets converted into XRP for the project — structured
selling, not surprise dumps.

**Crop Dusting.** The main event for holders: weekly bonus airdrops from
high-engagement partner projects, voted on by the community. Hold enough $FARM
and set the right trustlines, and dustings land in your wallet.

**Farm Assured Guarantee.** Their stated policy: never market-sell treasury
funds, and only pull XRP out of AMMs at 2x profit. It protects partner projects
and the price.

**How to qualify.** Hold **123,000 FARM** (or the LP equivalent — tokens parked
in AMM liquidity pools count). Then set trustlines from their cropdusting
table: they may send ANY token on the list, even for past events. You don't
need every trustline, but a missing one means a missed dusting. Check yours at
the trustline checker.

**Animal Fam.** The Union's NFT collection (a collab with Syn3rgy Art Lab) —
1,589 pieces, 79 XRP floor when last read (2026-09-30).
